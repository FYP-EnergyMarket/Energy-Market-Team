Energy Market

Edge based forecasting and Lifeline Power Buying System

The Lifeline Power Buying System is a software solution designed to connect battery energy storage owners with consumers who need essential power during outages. This platform enables reliable access to power during outages and provides Battery Energy Storage System(BESS) owners with monetization opportunities. When it comes to efficently managing distributed energy resources(DERs) like Battery energy storage systems, an accurate forecast of the predicted energy usage can be really useful. We have developed a forecasting model that does this. Furthermore, in rural areas, where cloud connectivity is not guaranteed, running inference on the cloud will not always be possible. Therefore, we have quantized this model and deployed it in an edge device for edge inference.
